Good to know

Questions people ask.

When do the tours leave?

Every Friday and Tuesday. The Bakreswar, Tarapith and Bolpur tour leaves at 6 AM. The North Bengal tour leaves Esplanade at 8 PM by AC luxury bus.

What does the price include?

Stay at a deluxe hotel, resort or holiday home and four Hindu non-veg meals a day. The Birbhum tour is ₹3,500 per head plus GST. Ask on WhatsApp for the North Bengal price and the full list of inclusions. T&C apply.

Can I come with a big group?

Yes. The tours are made for families and friends. For a large group, or to plan a trip to another place, send Dilip a message with your dates and group size.

How do I pay?

Send your booking on WhatsApp first. Dilip will confirm seats and share the advance amount and payment details.

Is vegetarian food available?

The standard menu is Hindu non-veg. Tell Dilip when you book if anyone in your group needs vegetarian meals.
